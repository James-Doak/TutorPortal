Privacy policy

How TutorPortal handles your information

Last updated 10 November 2026

What we collect

When you create a profile we collect your email address, a display name and a password (stored only in hashed form). If you sign in with Google we receive your name and email address from Google. We do not receive your Google password.

We also store the practice papers you build (their title, the questions chosen and whether you marked them completed) so they appear in your History.

How we use it

Your information is used only to let you sign in, to keep your paper history, and to run the site. We do not sell your information, share it with advertisers or use it for marketing.

Where it is stored

Accounts and saved papers are stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Each user can only access their own saved papers.

Your choices

You can delete any paper from your History at any time. To have your account and all its data deleted, contact us at the address below.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: tutorportalapp@gmail.com
